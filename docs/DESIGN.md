# Rahat design reference (frontend)

Prototype. Not an official service. All identity, payment and data services are simulated.

The rules both frontend tracks follow. Citizen app (`frontend/src/citizen`, `frontend/src/shared`, Track D1); staff console (`frontend/src/staff`, Track D2). Change tokens, pictograms or shared components by request to D1.

## Visual language v2: "Noor" (decided Sat 21 Nov, 20:00; supersedes the "never gradients" rule below)

The owner asked for an interface that stops people in their tracks: judges, citizens and the team. The answer
is not decoration. It is one idea told everywhere: **light reaching the last home.** Every family is a point of
light; Rahat is the thread that reaches the last one; a decision is a lamp being lit, a scam is a light being
shielded, "I am not sure" is a lamp handed to a person.

**Sources.** The Sindhi ajrak block print (indigo, madder red, black, ivory; the eight-point star, the khatam)
and a Karachi night sky. Nothing here is a copy of an existing product.

**Palette** (`shared/tokens.css`). Paper theme for citizens: ivory `paper`, parchment `surface`, indigo-black
`ink`. Meaning colours: `primary` emerald (relief, yes, paid), `danger` madder (scam, stop), `warning` saffron
(we need something from you), `info` indigo (a person will help). Light: `noor` lamp gold, only for the moment
something good happens (approved, paid, verified). Night palette `night`..`night-3`, `night-ink`,
`night-muted`, and bright `emerald` / `emerald-glow` / `madder-glow` / `indigo-glow` for night surfaces.

**Geometry.** The emblem (`shared/components/Emblem.tsx`): two squares make the eight-point star, a lamp at the
centre, eight homes around it; it draws itself (`animate`). The `rh-lattice` / `rh-lattice-night` texture
repeats the star as a faint background. Corners: `rounded-xl` / `rounded-2xl` for cards, `rounded-full` for
pills. Depth only with meaning: `shadow-lift` (raised, tappable), `shadow-float` (sheets, dialogs),
`shadow-glow*` (light: success, live, focus of attention).

**Type.** System fonts only. Latin: system-ui, heavy weights (700 to 900) and tight tracking for display
(`text-4xl`, `text-5xl`, `text-hero`), `rh-num` tabular numbers that count up. Urdu: Nastaliq from the device,
never squeezed: display sizes keep a line height of at least 1.6. Monospace for hashes and codes.

**Motion** (tokens `--ease-out`, `--ease-spring`, `animate-rise|fade|pop|draw|twinkle|ring|float|spin-slow|shimmer`;
classes `rh-stagger`, `rh-draw`, `rh-skeleton`; route changes use the View Transitions API). Rules:
- Motion explains: it shows where something came from, what changed, what to look at next. 120 ms feedback,
  220 ms state change, 420 ms entrance, up to 1.2 s for a story moment (a decision, a payment).
- Animate transform and opacity only (60 fps on a cheap phone). No layout thrash, no scroll hijacking.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: everything becomes a short fade; nothing loops. Respect `Save-Data` for heavy work.
- Sound and touch are part of motion on the citizen app: a soft generated chime (Web Audio, no files) and a
  short vibration on a decision, both off when the phone is silent or the person turned them off.

**Signature moments** (each built once, reused): the self-drawing emblem; the journey path where each step is a
star that lights up; the verdict reveal (approved: the lamp lights and the homes glow; needs proof: a saffron
card slides in; a person will check: the lamp is handed over); the scam shield; the live pulse of the system.

**Where weight is allowed.** The citizen route keeps its 100 KB gzip budget: CSS, SVG and tiny scripts only.
WebGL (three.js) lives only in its own lazy chunk on `/live`, loads only when WebGL works and neither
reduced motion nor Save-Data is on, and always has a static SVG fallback.

**Still never.** Emoji as icons, web fonts, icon packages, placeholder text, numbers or claims the live system
cannot back, motion that hides content, text below AA contrast in either theme.

## Users and reading level

- **Citizen**: a widow who cannot read, a shopkeeper, a helper with 40 families. A cheap Android phone, weak signal, often offline. Urdu by default (RTL), and English. Sindhi was dropped: nobody on the team can proofread it, and an unchecked translation is worse than none.
- Copy is grade 5: at most three short sentences, everyday words, no jargon, no marketing. Each string is a key in `shared/i18n/{en,ur}.ts`; the compiler checks Urdu has every English key.
- Every citizen key without a `{param}` has a recording at `public/audio/{lang}/{key}.mp3` saying the same thing, generated by `node scripts/make-audio.mjs` (edge-tts, `ur-PK-UzmaNeural` and `en-IN-NeerjaNeural`) and committed. `public/audio/{lang}/index.json` lists them. The script regenerates only changed text; where the screen shows digits (`15,000`, `0999`) it speaks words (`SPOKEN` in the script). A team member's own recording can replace any file under the same name. Missing files fail silently: a device voice for that language if one exists, else silence. Never read Urdu with an English voice.
- The server's own fixed sentences are recorded too, under the audio keys the API names (`scam.reason.asks_fee`, `scam.certainty.likely_scam`, `scam.todo.*`, `reason.<code>`, `next.<code>`): `node scripts/server-prompts.mjs` snapshots them from the backend into `scripts/server-prompts.json` (committed), and `make-audio.mjs` records that file as well. Re-run both when the backend's wording changes. A screen plays the server's clips only when every one exists (`useServerSpeech`), otherwise its sentences with a device voice.
- **Staff**: officers, admins, auditors on a laptop. English, LTR, dense.

## Pictograms (`shared/components/Pictogram.tsx`)

One hand-drawn set: 48x48 grid, 3px round strokes, `currentColor`. Names: `yes`, `no`, `unknown` (I do not know), `speaker`, `back`, `forward`, `person-help` (a person will help), `document`, `card` (the Rahat card), `payment`, `income-1` to `income-4` (one to four banknotes: the income bands), `family`, `widow`, `disability`, `phone`, `warning`, `retry`, `offline`, `lock`, `person`, `delete`, `home`, `clock`, `camera`, `microphone`, `plus`, `minus`, `edit`.

Phase 2: `message` (a text message) and its verdicts `msg-scam` (cross), `msg-warn` (!), `msg-unsure` (?), `msg-ok` (tick); `shield` (the scam checker), `shield-ok` (really sent by Rahat); `link`; `paste` (clipboard); `pause` (stopped for now); `less` (share less); `phone-saved` (saved on this phone); `sending`. `CrossedPictogram` draws any picture with a red slash for "do not" (do not pay, do not share your PIN) and for data no longer shared.

Tracking-code symbols (the backend's `TRACKING_SYMBOLS`, spoken as `sym_{name}`): `moon`, `star`, `fish`, `tree`, `house`, `sun`, `bird`, `flower`, `key`, `boat`, `lamp`, `leaf`, `cup`, `drum`, `kite`, `bell`.

- A picture named by the server (`explanation.pictogram`, `tracking_symbols`) goes through `pictogramFor()`, which also knows a few aliases (`person-will-help`); an unknown name falls back to the outcome's own picture.

- Draw new ones on the same grid and stroke; add them to the set, not inline.
- `back`, `forward` and `delete` mirror in RTL. Inside an LTR island (keypad, phone number) pass `mirror={false}`: Tailwind's `rtl:` matches any RTL ancestor.
- Put a word next to the icon where there is room. Icon-only buttons need `aria-label`.

## Citizen rules

- One question per screen, one primary action, a way back (top bar, start side), a speaker button (top bar, end side) that plays the screen's prompt.
- The first screen has one large start button; its tap unlocks audio (browsers block sound before a tap). After that, prompts may auto-play.
- Tap targets at least 48px; answers and keys 64px. No typing needed to finish the application. Numbers use the keypad component.
- Never speak a PIN digit aloud. Phone digits may be spoken.
- Mock data only: phone fixed to `0999-` + 7 digits in the UI; IDs `00000-NNNNNNN-C`.
- Show detection details (why something was flagged) only in staff and auditor views. Citizens hear "a person will check".
- Render untrusted text (names, OCR, messages) as text, never as HTML. No `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`.
- Budget: citizen JS at most 100 KB gzip, aim for 60. `npm run check:bundle` fails the build above 100 KB or if staff code leaks into the citizen entry.

## Staff rules

- Dense and tabular: `text-sm`, `size="sm"` buttons (32px is fine on desktop), tables with sticky headers, no decorative chrome.
- Keyboard first: real links and buttons, visible focus, skip link, native `<select>`, `Enter` submits.
- Navigation comes from the role (`staff/nav.ts`) and is cosmetic: the server enforces access. Every page handles 403 with the permission-denied state.
- Unbuilt pages are dev placeholders marked `built: false`; build with `VITE_SHOW_UNBUILT=false` to hide them before the demo.
- Staff code lives in the lazy `staff` chunk; `/demo` in the lazy `demo` chunk. Never import `src/staff/**` from citizen or shared code.

## State checklist (every screen)

| State | What the person sees | Helper |
|---|---|---|
| Loading | spinner and "Please wait" (no blank screen, no layout jump) | `ScreenState kind="loading"` |
| Empty | what is missing and what to do next | `kind="empty"` |
| Error | plain message, Try again, `request_id` as a reference | `kind="error"`, `FailureState` |
| Offline | the network banner (always) plus what still works; never lose typed work | `NetworkBanner`, `kind="offline"` |
| Permission denied | "not for you", who can help; never hide the cause | `kind="denied"` |
| Not found | where to go instead | `kind="not_found"` |
| Success | the screen itself, with the next step | |

Wrap calls with `call(api.GET(...))` (`shared/api/problem.ts`): it never throws and maps failures to a kind. 401 is handled for you (one refresh, one retry); a session that cannot be refreshed signs the person out.

## RTL and language rules

- `<html lang dir>` follows the language signal. Use logical utilities only: `ms-/me-`, `ps-/pe-`, `start-/end-`, `text-start/end`, `border-s/e`. Never `ml-`, `pr-`, `left-`, `text-left`.
- Urdu font stack, from the device: `"Noto Nastaliq Urdu", "Jameel Noori Nastaleeq", "Noto Naskh Arabic", system-ui`. Line height is at least 2 for Urdu; it is set per language, so do not use fixed `leading-*` or fixed heights on text (use `min-h-*` and padding).
- A run in another language gets its own `lang` and `dir` (for example the English footer line).
- Numbers, phones, PINs and IDs: Western digits, wrapped in `rh-ltr` (direction ltr, bidi isolate). `t()` wraps every `{param}` in Unicode isolates, so names and numbers do not scramble the sentence.

## Security rules in the client

- Access token in memory only (`shared/api/session.ts`). Never `localStorage`, `sessionStorage` or IndexedDB for tokens or personal data, with one exception: unsent work sealed in the vault (AES-GCM, key from the PIN; see "Offline" below). The refresh token is an httpOnly cookie the script never sees; a reload calls `/v1/auth/refresh` once.
- Strict CSP (`script-src 'self'; style-src 'self'`): no inline scripts, `<style>` tags or `style="..."` in HTML. `npm run preview` sends the same policy.
- The service worker precaches the app shell (the lazy scam and consent chunks included) and the silent unlock clip only. Voice prompts are cached at runtime, cache-first, for `/audio/` only (a missing file answered with the app shell is not cached: the content type must be `audio/*`); the page warms the chosen language in the background after the first screen (`warmAudio`, one file at a time, skipped with Save-Data). Prompts of screens that need the network anyway (scam checker, consent centre, warnings, server explanations) are not warmed; they are cached the first time they play. No other runtime route exists, so an API response is never cached.
- Every API request carries `Accept-Language` with the UI language, so the server can answer in it.

## Citizen application flow (D1.3, D1.4)

| Path | Screen |
|---|---|
| `/apply/consent` | a. what we use, what for, can be taken back; Yes creates the consent and then the application (`client_op_id` and `Idempotency-Key`) |
| `/apply/declined` | No: a kind ending, nothing is stored |
| `/apply/card` | b. photo of the Rahat card: camera (`capture`), file picker as fallback; resized on the device to at most 300 KB (EXIF dropped); the server's `retake_hint` or `message_key` is shown and spoken; after three tries "a person will help" and the flow goes on |
| `/apply/name` | c. the name read from the card (`ocr` or `qr_signed` only). Yes writes nothing (a tapped value must not replace a signed one); No clears it so a person checks |
| `/apply/household`, `/income`, `/widow`, `/disability`, `/aid` | d to h. One question, pictogram answers, the chosen answer is read back; "I do not know" sends `value: null` |
| `/apply/review` | i. answers as icons, read aloud, per-answer save status, one Send |
| `/result`, `/track`, `/ask`, `/proof` | outcome and explanation from the server; tracker with symbols, timeline and latest news; ask a person; a photo of the proof asked for. An application paused by a withdrawn consent offers "Share again" |

- These paths are matched in `citizen/routes.tsx` (the router's default route), so `App.tsx` does not change.
- Every write goes through the outbox (below): consent, create, each answer (`PATCH` with `base_version`, provenance `tapped`, never `verified`), each photo, and Send. Online it empties at once; offline the person goes on answering and every item shows its status. `409 consent_required` returns to the consent screen; `409 consent_scope_missing` (the person shared less) marks that answer "You chose not to share this. A person will check it instead."
- The draft is in memory and sealed in the vault next to the outbox, so an offline reload can show it again; it is wiped from memory on sign-out, a person change and the idle lock. After an online reload it is read from the server with the answers still queued on top.
- Server keys are mapped to ours by `messageKeyFor()` (`timeline.submitted` to `timeline_submitted`); only known keys are shown. Server sentences are shown as text in the language they were written in (`explanation.lang`), with their own `lang` and `dir`.

## Helper Mode (D1.9)

One shared phone, up to 40 families. A helper signs in with their own phone and PIN and lands on the family list (`citizen/helper/HelperFamilies.tsx`, a lazy chunk): large cards with the name in Urdu and English, the district, the application status as a picture, whether consent is on, whether the delegation is live; search by name or place; "Show more families" pages the list.

1. Tapping a live family asks for **that family's PIN** (`FamilyPin`, never the helper's). `POST /v1/helper/switch` returns a ticket. A wrong PIN shows "tries left" from `attempts_left`; a lock (`429 pin_locked`) shows the wait and shuts the keypad until it ends; `pin_blocked`, `pin_not_set` and `no_delegation` each have their own plain sentence. PIN digits are never spoken.
2. From then on `shared/api/client.ts` adds `X-Acting-For` and `X-Helper-Ticket` to every request except `/v1/helper/*` and `/v1/auth/*` (`shared/acting.ts` holds them, in memory only). The **banner** "Helping {name}" with "Change family" is the first thing in `CitizenShell`, so it is on every screen.
3. Inside the family the normal screens are used (apply, tracker, scam checker, rules, My information). The server decides what the delegation's scopes allow; a 403 shows the permission-denied state. Helpers do not see "Sign out" while acting.
4. **Wipe.** "Change family", 3 minutes without a touch (`ACTING_IDLE_MS`) and the end of the ticket (15 minutes) all run `endActing()` (`citizen/actingControl.ts`): the family's unsent work is sealed, `acting` is cleared, `wipePerson()` runs every registered clean-up (draft, outbox, consents, scam answers, warnings), audio stops, and `POST /v1/helper/switch/end` stops the ticket. `wipePerson()` runs the session's end handlers except the vault key, so the helper's own vault stays open. A response that arrives for a family already left is thrown away (`actingEpoch` in `shared/api/client.ts`), so it cannot be stored for the next one. Idle and expiry bring the helper back to the list with a sentence saying why.
5. **Separate queues.** The outbox and the draft are sealed in the helper's vault under `outbox:{person}` and `draft:{person}`; opening a family restores only its own and sends it with its own ticket (`restoreSealed`). Unsent answers of a family stay sealed until the helper opens that family again.
6. A reload or the 10 minute lock drops the family (memory only); the helper types their own PIN and sees the list.

## Voice answers (D1.6)

Each question screen (household, income, widow, disability, other aid) has "Say your answer" above its pictures (lazy chunk `voice/VoiceAnswer.tsx`; hidden when the phone has no speech recognition). The phone's own recogniser hears it, the transcript goes to `POST /v1/voice/interpret` with the question key and the type it expects, and the answer comes back as a read-back sentence with `audio_keys`. The screen shows the answer as a picture (the number with that many people, the income picture, yes, no or "I do not know") and the sentence, plays the server's clips (a device voice for any clip we do not have, never Urdu with an English voice) and asks "Yes, that is right" or "No, say it again". Only the yes icon writes it: through the outbox, provenance `spoken`, with the confidence the server gave. `fallback` is followed: `say_again` shows the server's sentence and the microphone again; `use_pictures` hides the microphone. Permission denied or no recogniser says so and leaves the pictures; offline or a failed call says so and leaves the pictures; `429` says to wait. The pictures are always there.

## Ask about the rules (D1.12)

`/rules` (home, "Ask about the rules"; lazy chunk `ask/RulesScreen.tsx`). Speak (read back with a device voice, then "Ask this") or type, `POST /v1/ask`. `shared/i18n/more.en.ts` and `more.ur.ts` hold the strings of Helper Mode, voice, rules and the rest of My information; `shared/i18n/more.ts` registers them when a lazy chunk imports it. `t()` finds them there; the compiler checks the Urdu file against the English one. `make-audio.mjs` records them too. ## Offline: the outbox and the vault (D1.5, D1.10)

- **Outbox** (`citizen/apply/outbox.ts`): every write is an op, sent in order, one at a time: `consent` (Idempotency-Key), `create` (`client_op_id`, also the Idempotency-Key), `patch` (`base_version`), `upload` (Idempotency-Key), `submit` (one Idempotency-Key for every try). Consent and create go to the front; answers given while a patch waits are merged into it.
- **Never twice.** A `409 version_conflict` re-reads the application, drops the answers the server already has (a lost response) and resends the rest on the new version. An upload counts the documents of its kind first; after a lost response the next try finds the photo that arrived instead of sending it again. Create and submit are idempotent on the server.
- **Statuses** per item: "Saved on this phone" (sealed), "Not sent yet" (memory only: no key), "Sending", "Sent", or a plain failure. The question screens, the review and the home screen show them; the network banner is always visible offline.
- **Transport.** The queue talks to a `Transport` (`send(op)`, outcomes `done | again | wait | retry | drop`). Today that is the REST API (`flow.ts`); the batched `POST /v1/sync` can replace it without touching the queue: an op already has `op_id`, `kind`, the entity, `base_version`, the patch and `created_at`.
- **Vault** (`shared/vault.ts`): the queue, the photos and the draft are sealed in IndexedDB with AES-GCM 256. The key is derived from the PIN at sign-in (PBKDF2-SHA-256, 150,000 iterations, a random 16-byte salt per vault), is not extractable and lives in memory only. Each record has its own IV and is bound to its vault and name as additional data. A vault is found by a hash of the user id; the phone number and name are only inside its sealed check record.
- **Reload.** The key never survives one: a signed-in citizen or helper with a vault types the PIN (`UnlockScreen`) and the queue resumes. With no network the PIN signs in on the phone: it opens the vault (the sealed phone number must match) and the app runs from it with no token; when the network returns the session is refreshed from the cookie and the queue goes.
- **Lock and wipe.** After 10 minutes without a touch the key, the token and everything decrypted are wiped from memory and the PIN is asked again (`shared/idle.ts`); the sealed queue stays, so nothing is lost. Five wrong PINs delete the vault. Sign-out deletes it, after a warning when items are not sent yet; "This is not me" signs out and keeps the owner's sealed answers.
- **Honest limit.** A 4 to 6 digit PIN can be guessed offline by someone who copies the browser's storage off the phone. The vault keeps the data unreadable to other people using the phone and to casual inspection, not to a forensic attacker. A real service would add a key share held by the server or bound to the device.
