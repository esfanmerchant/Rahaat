## The steps {#steps}

To apply, sign in with a phone number and a PIN, and give permission to use the information.
Then take a photo of the front of the Rahat card and answer the short questions about the household.
Last, check the answers and press the send button.

## Answering by voice {#voice}

Every question can be answered by speaking or by tapping a picture.
After a spoken answer, the app says the answer back and asks if it is right.
If the app does not understand, it asks again, and after three tries it shows the pictures.

## Help from a helper {#helpers}

A community helper can fill the application for a family, after the family gives the helper permission.
A helper uses their own PIN and never the family's PIN.

## No internet {#offline}

The app keeps the answers on the phone when there is no internet.
The answers are sent when the internet comes back, so nothing is lost when the connection drops.

## Permission to use information {#consent}

Rahat uses the name, ID card, phone, household and income only to decide the relief payment.
Permission can be taken back at any time in the app.
When permission is taken back, staff can no longer see the information.
