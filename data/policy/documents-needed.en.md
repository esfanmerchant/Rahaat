## The Rahat card {#rahat-card}

The Rahat card is the only document needed to start an application.
The app needs a photo of the front of the card.
The card has a signed QR code that confirms the name and the ID number.

## A good photo {#photo-tips}

Put the card on a flat place in good light and show the whole card in the photo.
Move away from a lamp or the sun if the card shines.
A blurry or cut photo is never a rejection, and the app asks for a new photo.

## Proof of income {#income-proof}

A photo of a proof of income is only needed when the app or a person asks for it.
A salary slip or a letter from an employer can be a proof of income.

## If there is no Rahat card or the photo does not work {#no-card}

A household that does not have a Rahat card can visit the Rahat office or ask a helper.
After three tries with the photo, a person helps with the card.
