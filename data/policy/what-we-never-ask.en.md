## Things Rahat never asks for {#never}

Rahat never asks for a fee, any money, a PIN, or a one-time code sent by SMS.
Rahat never asks anyone to install another app.
Rahat never asks for a photo of the card on WhatsApp.

## Staff and helpers {#staff}

Rahat staff and helpers never ask for money, and a helper never needs the family's PIN.
Anyone who asks for money in the name of Rahat should be reported.
