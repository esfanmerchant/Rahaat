## How to appeal {#appeal}

An applicant who disagrees with a decision can appeal within 30 days.
To appeal, press "Ask a person" in the app or visit the Rahat office.
A different officer looks at the appeal, and an appeal costs nothing.

## What to tell the officer {#what-to-say}

Give the tracking code of the application and say which answer or decision seems wrong.
Bring any paper that shows the right information.

## Wrong information on the card {#correction}

Wrong information on the Rahat card is fixed with a correction request.
A person checks the correction before the record is changed.

## Complaints {#complaint}

A complaint about a staff member or a helper can be made in the app or at the office.
Every complaint is recorded and a person answers it.
