## Signs of a fake message {#signs}

A message that asks for a fee to release relief money, or for a PIN or a code, is fake.
A link to an unknown website or a sender that is an ordinary mobile number is a warning sign.
A message that says to hurry or lose the money is a warning sign.

## A real Rahat message {#real-message}

Real Rahat messages come from the sender name RAHAT.
Every real Rahat message ends with a verify code, and a real message never asks for money.

## What to do with a suspicious message {#what-to-do}

Do not reply to the message and do not pay anything.
Check the message in the app's message checker, and report it so that other people are warned.
Ask a helper or visit the Rahat office when unsure.
