## The tracking code {#tracking-code}

Every application gets a tracking code with three pictures and a number.
The code can be shown to a helper or at the Rahat office to find the application.

## What the status means {#status}

"A person is checking" means an officer is looking at the application.
"One more paper needed" means a photo of one more document is needed.
"Money sent" means the payment has been sent to the mock wallet.

## Approved or not approved {#outcome}

"Approved" means the rules are met and the payment can be sent.
"Not approved" means a rule is not met, and the decision can be appealed within 30 days.

## Messages from Rahat {#messages}

Rahat sends a message at each step of the application.
Every real Rahat message ends with a short verify code.
The verify code can be checked in the app's message checker.

## How long it takes {#time}

Most applications get a decision within a few days.
Applications that a person must check can take longer.
The app shows each step as it happens.
